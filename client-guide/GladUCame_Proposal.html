What we see in your inbox

Glad U Came does not get one kind of message. In six days the concierge sorted the inbox into very different people, and each needs a different answer:

Brands with a budget

Brand leads asking for campaigns, creators and reach. These are the revenue. They need a quick, confident reply, a budget range and a contact captured, then your team on the call.

Creators you invited

Creators answering your outreach, and creators pitching themselves. Many ask "is it paid or barter?", which is a conversation your team should negotiate, not lose in a busy inbox.

Everyone else

Vendors, promos, job seekers, auto-replies and people chasing for an update. The concierge already tells them apart so your team's time goes to the right 20%.

The problem we are solving next

Conversations are split across apps

A brand starts on Instagram, then says "let's continue on WhatsApp". From that moment the history, the budget they mentioned and the promise your team made live in two places, and the second one has no memory at all.

No single view of the pipeline

Today, to know "which brands are warm this week?", someone scrolls chats. There is no list of who is waiting on us, who went quiet, who asked for a call and who is ready to sign.
