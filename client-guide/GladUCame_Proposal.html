Who writes to you

In six days on Instagram we saw very different people, and each needs a different reply.

Brands

They want a campaign, creators or reach. This is where the revenue is. They need a fast reply, their budget range and a phone number, and then a call with your team.

Creators

Some you invited, some wrote to you first. Many ask if the work is paid or barter. Your team should handle that talk, so it must not get buried.

Everyone else

Vendors, promotions, job seekers, auto-replies and people asking for an update. The agent already tells these apart so your team can focus on brands and creators.

What is not working yet

Enquiries sit in four places

A brand may fill the website form, then message on Instagram, then move to WhatsApp. Each app only knows its own part of the story. Nobody sees all of it.

Nobody knows the numbers

How many enquiries came in this week? Which place sends the most brands? Today you would have to count by hand. There is no list of who is waiting, who went quiet and who is ready to sign.

What we suggest

Keep the Instagram agent as it is. Add WhatsApp, the website chat and the website form. Send everything into one CRM that your team opens every morning to see who to talk to first, and how many enquiries came in.
